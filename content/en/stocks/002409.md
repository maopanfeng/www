**Jiangsu Yoke Technology Co., Ltd. (SZSE: 002409)**

This article uses information disclosed by July 1, 2026. The growth judgments below are the author's analysis and are not company guidance or a performance promise.

## Long-term growth perspective

#### 1. Core logic: Can a materials supplier keep participating in customers' technology upgrades?

The key businesses to watch are electronic materials and LNG insulation panels. The company's annual report says its semiconductor precursors are used in memory and logic chips, and that it advances product adoption through joint development, testing and customer qualification. This business is best understood by connecting each material with the customer's manufacturing process. [Source: 2025 Annual Report, Section III](https://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2026-04-28/1225216877.PDF)

The investment hypothesis is that a supplier can turn research spending into durable revenue only when a material repeatedly passes validation in a customer's next-generation process and becomes a stable purchase. The question is whether this conversion can happen repeatedly, rather than whether one product has entered a popular theme.

Competitive strength can be tested with three questions: Has a new product generated mass-production revenue? Can existing products maintain profitability? How much capital is required for incremental revenue? Customer qualification is the starting evidence; sustained deliveries, profit and cash collection provide the later validation.

LNG panels should be assessed separately for order execution and cash collection. Looking only at total revenue can hide the source of growth in each business. The growth thesis should rest on evidence by business line.

#### 2. Financial verification: Does profit and cash follow revenue growth?

In 2025, revenue was RMB 8.611 billion, up 25.49%; net profit attributable to shareholders was RMB 1.000 billion, up 14.77%; net profit attributable to shareholders excluding non-recurring items was RMB 954 million, up 5.91%; and net cash flow from operating activities was RMB 1.031 billion. [Source: 2025 Annual Report, page 7](https://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2026-04-28/1225216877.PDF)

These figures support an expansion in scale, but profit grew more slowly than revenue. They do not yet prove that scale advantages have fully translated into returns. Product mix, costs and expenses need to be checked to see how much profit incremental revenue produces.

In the first quarter of 2026, revenue was RMB 1.973 billion, down 6.85% year over year; net profit attributable to shareholders was RMB 267 million, up 2.47%; net profit attributable to shareholders excluding non-recurring items was RMB 244 million, down 3.81%; and net cash flow from operating activities was negative RMB 42.36 million, compared with negative RMB 32.16 million a year earlier. [Source: 2026 First-Quarter Report, page 2](https://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2026-04-28/1225216885.PDF)

The different directions of reported and adjusted profit are a reminder to separate core operations from non-recurring items. Quarterly cash flow also needs to be read with the timing of receipts and payments; it should not be extrapolated directly to the full year. Long-term growth should be tested against revenue, adjusted profit and cash flow together.

#### 3. Falsification: What could weaken the growth thesis?

The first risk is that technology spending does not become commercial returns. If new products remain in testing for too long, or lack adequate profitability after entering production, research capability has not yet become shareholder value. The focus should be verifiable production and sales progress.

The second risk is that growth consumes too much capital. If revenue expands while receivables, inventory and capital expenditure keep rising, the quality of growth needs to be reassessed. Operating cash flow after capital expenditure helps show whether the business can support its own expansion.

The final risk is the expectation embedded in the price. Even if the business develops as expected, an excessive purchase price can reduce investment returns. This article does not build a valuation model and therefore does not provide a target price or a buy recommendation.

The question worth continuing to study is whether Yoke Technology can repeatedly turn materials research into customer purchases, and revenue into cash returns. As of the information date used here, annual growth and first-quarter slowing coexist; the thesis should be updated as new evidence arrives.
